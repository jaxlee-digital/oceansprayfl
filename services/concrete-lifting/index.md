Florida's sandy fill and high water table make concrete settlement
almost inevitable over time. When the soil beneath a slab loses
density — from water movement, compaction failure, or voids forming
below — the concrete above it follows. The slab is usually fine.
The soil is the problem.

**Polyurethane injection lifts concrete back to grade by expanding
beneath the slab and filling the void that caused the settling.**
No demolition. No concrete trucks. Usable in 15 minutes.

## Why foam lifting beats demo and repour

| | Polyurethane Injection | Demo & Repour |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost** | About half | Full price |
| **Time** | Hours | Days to weeks |
| **Usable** | 15 minutes | Cure window |
| **Demolition** | None | Full tear-out |
| **Mess** | Minimal | Significant |

The only time demo and repour is the right answer: when the concrete
itself is structurally compromised — cracked through, rebar exposed
and rusted, or so deteriorated it won't hold together under lift
pressure. We'll tell you honestly if that's the case.
