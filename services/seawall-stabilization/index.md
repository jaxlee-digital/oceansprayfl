Most failing seawalls in Southwest Florida don't need replacement.
They need the soil behind them fixed.

In SWFL, tidal cycling, boat wakes, and groundwater movement pull
fine-grained fill out from behind seawall panels over years.
Voids form. The wall loses support. From the surface it looks
like the wall is failing — cracks, settlement, leaning. The real
failure is underground.

**Polyurethane injection fills those voids, binds the soil, and
restores the structural support your wall needs — without
demolition, without a marine contractor, at about half the cost
of replacement.**
