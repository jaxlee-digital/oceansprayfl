A sinking driveway, a dropping apron, or standing water at the
garage threshold almost never means the slab needs replacement.
The slab is fine. The fill underneath has compressed.

**Polyurethane injection lifts the driveway back to grade by
expanding under the slab and filling the void that caused the
settling.** No demo, no concrete pour, no waiting. You drive on it
the same day.

## How it works

1. **Free on-site assessment.** We measure deflection, identify
   the underlying cause, and provide a written quote.
2. **Engineered lift plan.** We mark injection points and
   calculate resin volume per panel.
3. **Day-of injection.** Small ports drilled, resin injected in
   stages, slab rises back to level in real time.
4. **Patch and clean.** Ports patched flush, area cleaned, drive
   on it immediately.

## Concrete and pavers

We lift poured concrete, stamped concrete, exposed aggregate, and
the base under paver driveways. Pavers themselves can be lifted
by re-bedding the sand course, but if the underlying base or
subgrade has settled, polyurethane injection under the base is
the durable fix.

## When to call

- Apron dropped at the street or at the garage threshold
- Standing water at the garage door or at the apron
- Trip hazards at expansion joints
- Differential settlement between adjacent panels
- Slab intact but no longer flat or sloped correctly
