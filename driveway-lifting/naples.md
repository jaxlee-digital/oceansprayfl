  - **Pavers bird-bathing or rolling** as the underlying base has
    settled
  - **Edge-restraint failure** at the apron
  - **Apron drop at the street** in older neighborhoods with
    decades of seasonal soil swing

  **Pavers vs. concrete:**

  Pavers themselves can be lifted by re-bedding the sand course,
  but if the underlying base or subgrade has settled, polyurethane
  injection under the base is the durable fix. We work with both
  approaches.
