local_callout: |
  We work pool decks across the full Naples spectrum, from 1960s
  Port Royal estates to 2020s Bay Colony and Pelican Bay builds.
  The failure modes change by neighborhood, but the fix is almost
  always the same: identify the void, lift the slab, leave the
  property looking untouched.

  **What we see on Naples pool deck assessments:**

  - **Settling toward the seawall** in Port Royal, Royal Harbor,
    Aqualane Shores, and Old Naples, often pointing back to soil
    loss behind the wall as the underlying cause
  - **Cage-footing drop** in Pelican Bay, Bay Colony, and the
    Vanderbilt Beach corridor where lanai-side footings have
    settled and screen panels are binding
  - **Coping separation** where the deck has dropped away from
    the pool shell
  - **Standing water** in spots that used to drain, especially in
    older Pine Ridge and Park Shore decks
  - **Trip hazards** at the lanai-to-deck transition

  **Naples pool deck materials we lift:**

  Plain broom-finish concrete, stamped concrete, travertine over
  slab, marble over slab, paver-set decks (we lift the underlying
  base; pavers are re-bedded by the homeowner's pool company or
  by us). We patch ports flush with color-matched compound; on
  textured or stamped surfaces the patches disappear within a
  visit or two of weathering.

  **Pair with seawall stabilization:**

  If your Naples pool deck has settled toward a seawall (Royal
  Harbor and the Gordon River corridor especially), the deck
  drop is a symptom and the seawall is the cause. We assess both
  during the visit. Lifting a deck without addressing an active
  seawall fill loss is a temporary fix; we tell you that up front
  and give you both numbers so you can decide.
