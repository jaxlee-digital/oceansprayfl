A sinking pool deck is rarely a deck problem. It's a soil problem.
The slab itself is almost always reusable. The fill underneath has
compressed, washed out, or settled, and the deck has followed it
down.

**Polyurethane injection lifts the deck back to grade by expanding
under the slab and filling the void that caused the settling.** No
demolition, no replacement pour, no waiting on concrete to cure.
Most pool decks are level again the same day, and you can walk on
it before we leave.

## How it works

1. **Free on-site assessment.** We measure deflection, identify
   the underlying cause, and quote per-section.
2. **Engineered lift plan.** We mark injection points, calculate
   resin volume, and identify the lift sequence to bring the
   deck back to level.
3. **Day-of injection.** Small ports drilled through the slab,
   resin injected in stages, deck rises back to grade in real
   time.
4. **Patch and clean.** Ports patched flush with color-matched
   compound, area cleaned, deck ready to use immediately.

## Materials we lift

Plain broom-finish concrete, stamped concrete, travertine and
marble over slab, paver-set decks. We patch ports flush with
color-matched compound; on textured surfaces the patches blend
into the panel within weeks.

## When to call

- Deck settled toward the seawall, the cage, or the lanai
- Trip hazards at the coping joint or expansion joints
- Cage footings dropped, screen panels binding
- Pool coping separated from the deck
- Standing water in spots that used to drain
- Slab intact but no longer level
