  - **Bay-side walls (East End to West End):** survived

  - **Captiva:** mixed. Damage profile varies dramatically by

    environment is strict. A poly injection job is land-side soil
    work and typically falls below the threshold that would
    trigger a full marine permit and review. Replacement triggers
    the full process
