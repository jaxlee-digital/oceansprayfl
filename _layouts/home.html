Why Polyurethane?

Lighter. Stronger. Made for the coast.

Lightweight

Won't sink into weak soil or add lateral pressure to a wall the way concrete does.

Eco-certified

Chemically inert after cure. Safe near marine ecosystems, recreational water, and drinking water sources.

Fast-curing

Resin sets in minutes. Most jobs are walk-on the same day with minimal site disruption.

About half the cost

Stabilization with polyurethane typically runs around 50% of what full seawall replacement would cost. It also avoids the demolition, permitting, and downtime that come with it.
